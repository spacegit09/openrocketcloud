Rocket in Cloud Refund Policy

Refund Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Every new account gets a free week of Rocket in Cloud with no card needed, so you can try everything before you pay. If you are not happy with a payment afterwards, you can have your money back.

14 days, for any reason

You can ask for a full refund within 14 days of any payment:

  • the OpenRocket plan (US$1), the Individual plan (US$5) or the Group plan (US$10);
  • extra places in a group (US$3 each);
  • each monthly payment of the Monthly plan (US$1).

You don’t need to give a reason. After 14 days, payments are not refunded, unless the law of your country says otherwise.

How to ask

  • Use the link in the receipt email Paddle sent you, or find your purchase at paddle.net; or
  • email us at flykim73@gmail.com with the email address you paid with and the date of the payment.

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders. Paddle provides all customer service inquiries and handles returns. The money goes back to the card or account you paid with; how long it takes to show depends on your bank.

What happens to your plan

  • A refunded Individual or Group plan ends when the refund is made. For a group, the people in it no longer have Rocket in Cloud through it.
  • Refunded extra places are taken off your group; the people added last lose their place first.
  • Refunding a monthly payment also cancels the Monthly plan.
  • Your work is kept either way: you can still see and download your designs and delete your work.

Cancelling a monthly plan

You can cancel the Monthly plan at any time on the Plan & billing page. It keeps working until the end of the month you paid for, and you are not charged again.

Your rights

This policy does not limit any rights you have under the law of your country. See also our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Rocketry safety

These features give estimates, not guarantees:

  • simulations

  • recovery, ejection charge and ballast calculations

Real flights can differ because of the weather, how the rocket was built, how the motor performs and other things the software can’t know. These estimates don’t replace:

  • the safety code of your rocketry organization (such as NAR or Tripoli) and the rules of your launch site;
  • the law and aviation rules where you fly, including airspace and permit requirements;
  • ground-testing your ejection charges and checking your recovery system before flight;
  • supervision by an experienced adult or certified flyer where it is required.

You are responsible for the safety of your rockets and flights.

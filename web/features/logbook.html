Logbook · every flight, kept

Every flight, on the record.

Keep each flight’s details, your altimeter’s data, photos and videos in one logbook — and see your real flight next to OpenRocket’s simulation and the Launch Planner’s prediction.

See how
Flight data

A fixed format, so mistakes can’t slip in.

Choose what your data has, and the logbook tells you exactly how to lay it out — then checks every line before anything is saved.

  • Time first, then altitude, velocity, acceleration or columns of your own, each in its unit
  • The exact column order, an example and a CSV template to download
  • Paste the text or open a CSV file — thousands of rows are fine
  • Every mistake listed by line: a missing value, a decimal comma, time going backwards
Prediction vs. reality

Your flight, OpenRocket and the planner — on one graph.

Bring in a design’s OpenRocket simulation and a Launch Planner plan with one click each. Every graph overlays every source of the same quantity, so you see at a glance how close the prediction came.

  • Altitude, velocity, acceleration and drift, each on its own graph
  • Shift a data set in time to line the curves up
  • Metric or imperial units, and a readout under the pointer
  • Highest and lowest values of every column in a table
  • Hide a data set, or download it again as CSV
Photos and videos

The pictures belong with the data.

Add the build photos, the launch video and the recovery shots to the flight they belong to, with a caption each.

  • Photos up to 50 MB and videos up to 100 MB
  • Stored privately, visible only to the people you share the logbook with
  • Each logbook’s card shows its photo or its altitude curve
Share and export

Share it with the team. Print it for the judges.

Logbooks are shared like designs, with editors and viewers. When you need it on paper, Export PDF puts it all in one clean report.

  • Details, notes, data, graphs and photos in one PDF
  • Saved as you type — there is nothing to forget
  • Prints any language, straight from your browser

How it fits together

From design to recovery, in one place.

Each tool hands its work to the next: the planner flies your OpenRocket design, and the logbook brings in both predictions.

01Design

Build and simulate in OpenRocket, with your team editing alongside you.

02Check

Size the parachute and ejection charge, add nose weight, check the fins for flutter.

03Plan

Fly it hundreds of times in the forecast for your field and see where it comes down.

04Log

Record the flight and lay your altimeter’s data over both predictions.

Real engineering

Serious numbers, made simple.

The Launch Planner flies OpenRocket’s own aerodynamics in NOAA’s forecast, fast enough to run hundreds of flights while you wait.

500flights flown for every launch plan, each with its own wind, thrust, drag and mass
31NOAA forecasts in the ensemble, so the landing area is as wide as the weather is uncertain
0.5%agreement with OpenRocket’s own apogee on its example designs
16 daysahead you can plan a launch, at your field’s exact spot
Built for teams and classrooms

Your work stays yours. Your team stays in sync.

Share by email

Invite teammates with their Google account as “Can edit” or “Can view”. Viewers watch every change live without touching the design.

Private by design

Designs, motors, launch plans and logbooks are stored encrypted. Only you and the people you share with can open them.

Nothing gets lost

Saving is continuous, every change is in the history, and a saved copy is kept every 10 minutes while people edit.

Keep your files

Upload any .ork file and download it again at any time. It opens in desktop OpenRocket as usual.

Works on school computers

Nothing to install and no admin rights needed: OpenRocket runs right in the browser.
